The first 20 days

What I do before anything is designed

Twenty days of structured observation, scoping and stakeholder alignment. The programme design comes after. This is what makes the difference between learning that lands and learning that fades.

01
Days 1 to 5

Listen, observe and map

  • Convene initial steering group with key stakeholders
  • Establish RACI and reporting lines from day one
  • Observe the operation: floors, calls, handovers, team meetings
  • Begin SEEN framework: Situation, Evidence, Effect, Need
  • Map the stakeholder landscape and identify decision makers
02
Days 6 to 10

Data gathering and root cause

  • TNA across roles, levels and business units
  • Skills scans against agreed competency framework
  • Fishbone analysis sessions with operational leads
  • Review existing data: attrition, NPS, performance, complaints
  • Identify the real priorities versus the stated ones
03
Days 11 to 15

Scoping and proof of concept

  • Present findings to steering group with evidence
  • Agree scope, priorities and what will not be in scope
  • Design proof of concept for highest priority area
  • Build milestone plan with clear evaluation touch points
  • Secure sign off on approach before anything is built
04
Days 16 to 20

Build and brief

  • Pilot proof of concept with a representative cohort
  • Debrief with participants and line managers
  • Finalise programme blueprint based on live learning
  • Brief internal teams and champions on their role
  • Set baseline metrics for behaviour and business impact

What you have after 20 days

A scoped, evidence based programme blueprint, with steering group sign off, a live proof of concept, baseline metrics and a milestone plan ready to run.
